On Paper Companion privacy

Updated September 24, 2026 · Available offline

On Paper Companion is published by HyperBuild, Inc. It helps you review and fill job applications using the On Paper desktop app. There is no developer-operated companion server, advertising, or extension telemetry. You stay in control of the final application submission.

Limited Use

On Paper Companion complies with the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. Information is used and transferred only for the disclosed application-assistance features. It is not sold, used for advertising, or used for credit or lending decisions. The developer has no access to your application data through an On Paper server.

When you choose a page

After you accept the first-use disclosure and request a scan, the extension reads the selected page’s address, form labels, input types, choices, and job context. It temporarily keeps the full tab address, including any query string and fragment, in extension memory to check that filling targets the reviewed tab and address. That full address is not saved to storage, included in application logs, or sent to the desktop app or AI provider. A separate origin-and-path address is used for job context comparisons inside the extension. URL credentials, raw page HTML, and live DOM nodes are not sent to the desktop app or AI provider.

Password fields are excluded. Sensitive questions, including demographics, disability, veteran status, work authorization, and compensation, require manual entry on the application page and are excluded from AI mapping and answer saving.

Your desktop app and optional AI

The extension communicates with your running On Paper app through an authenticated connection to 127.0.0.1 on your own computer. The app supplies the selected resume, profile, saved answers, suggestions, and requested resume PDF. Your OpenRouter API key stays in the app.

When you request AI suggestions, fit analysis, or tailoring, the app can send the selected resume, profile, saved answers, supported field descriptors, and job context to OpenRouter and its selected or fallback model provider. Your application URL is not included. These providers apply their own retention, training, security, and deletion policies. Review OpenRouter’s privacy policy and provider information. The desktop app separately asks for AI-sharing permission.

Filling and saving

Only reviewed values and a reviewed resume PDF are sent to the current application website when you choose to fill. The website may save edits or upload an attachment immediately, even before you submit. The extension never activates the site’s final Submit button. Answers and application records are saved to On Paper only when you request them.

iCloud sync

On supported Apple devices, On Paper can automatically sync workspace information, including resumes, profiles, saved answers, and application records, through Apple CloudKit in your own iCloud account. This is separate from the extension’s session storage. Apple’s terms and privacy policy apply; the developer does not receive these records through an On Paper server.

Pairing, consent, and deletion

The pairing credential is held only in memory-backed Chrome session storage, restricted to trusted extension contexts. It is cleared when Chrome restarts or the extension is reloaded, updated, or disabled. Your disclosure choice is stored locally in Chrome. Disconnect clears this extension’s session credential, disclosure choice, and current review, and asks the running app to revoke existing companion connections. If the app is unreachable, only this browser’s access is cleared; reconnect and disconnect while the app is running to revoke other sessions.

To prevent duplicate application records after an interruption, session storage also holds a random request identifier and a one-way fingerprint of the pending action. These are cleared after a confirmed save or when you disconnect.

Removing the extension removes its local settings. Delete saved answers or application records in On Paper, and manage synced copies through your iCloud storage settings. Disconnecting does not recall information already sent to an AI provider or application website, or erase saved app data, exports, and backups.

Support

The full On Paper policy is available at onpaper.pro/privacy.html. For support, email support@hyperbuild.com. Never send API keys or pairing tokens. You can also report non-private bugs through GitHub issues. Issues are public: do not post resumes or other private information.
