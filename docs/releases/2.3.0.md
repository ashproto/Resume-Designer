## On Paper 2.3.0

On Paper now brings iCloud sync to the Mac and improves how your resumes are kept and recovered. This update also prepares the desktop app for On Paper Companion, our upcoming Chrome extension.

- **iCloud sync on Mac.** Keep your resumes, profiles, jobs, and application records in sync through your own iCloud account. This also prepares On Paper for our upcoming iPhone and iPad app.
- **Companion is coming.** Adds support for reviewed autofill, application answers, and resume tailoring in Chrome on macOS. The extension will be released separately after Chrome Web Store approval.
- **Older resumes stay intact.** Fixes cases where sections in older resumes could disappear or change during editing.
- **Clearer resume recovery.** Keeps damaged resumes visible in your Library and explains how to recover their data. The Library’s Close button also works reliably again.
- **More reliable privacy controls.** Improves how On Paper remembers your choice to turn off AI sharing after a restart.
- **Backup download feedback.** Shows a confirmation when a backup download starts.
